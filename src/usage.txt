edits - give it a link, a topic or a file; get finished edits.

USAGE
  edits <youtube-link | topic | video-file> [options]
  edits doctor                 check ffmpeg, yt-dlp, whisper and the LLM command
  edits rate <clip.mp4> +|-    teach your taste: liked / disliked
  edits preset list | show NAME           presets are JSON: read one in full
  edits preset save FILE.json|-           save a changed preset (your folder wins over the built-in one)
  edits preset import <zip|xml|dir|prfpset>...   Alight Motion projects and Premiere presets into presets
  edits looks                  the colour looks
  edits mcp                    serve every command to an AI agent (MCP over stdio)
  edits skills [install]       agent skills for every command
  edits trends [country]      what is trending on TikTok now (default tr); use with --music trend:N
  edits study <folder>         measure edits you like; their shape, grade and loudness become the defaults

OPTIONS
      --music SONG    make music edits: the footage's best shots cut on the song's beats (link, file, or
                      trend:N for TikTok's Nth trending song, trend:us:N for another country)
      --preset NAME   replay a preset (name or .json file) over the footage: timing, speed, moves, shakes, flashes, song
      --intensity K   preset moves: 0.5 calmer, 2 wilder (default: the preset's own)
      --like EDIT     copy a reference edit: its music (dialogue removed), cuts, flashes, colour and shape
      --sad           slow cuts, slow motion, crossfades, black and white
      --voice         the speaker's best whole passage over their own footage, music under it, the drop as it ends, captions
      --say ID@A-B    use what that video says from A to B seconds (repeat for more edits); implies --voice
      --length S      seconds per music edit (default 18, sad 24)
      --tag TEXT      small signature at the bottom, like @yourname
      --text TEXT     a line over the whole music edit, in heavy capitals ("THE PROBLEM")
  -n, --moments N     clips (or music edits) to make, the best across all videos (default 3)
      --videos N      videos to look at for a topic or channel (default 5)
      --use K         music/preset edits: of those, read every one's captions and download only the best K (default 4)
      --aspect A      9:16 (default), 4:5, 1:1, 3:4, 4:3, 16:9 or 21:9
      --size WxH      any frame size, like 1080x1350
      --fit           keep the whole frame over a blurred copy instead of cropping around faces
      --look L        colour: none, bw, noir, dark, teal, tealorange, warm, cold, faded, vintage, sepia, bleach,
                      neon, matrix, sunset, pop (--grade works too)
      --lut FILE      a .cube LUT over the picture
      --lang L        spoken language for captions (default tr)
  -o, --out DIR       output folder (default ./edits-out)
      --join          also join all clips into joined.mp4
      --captions S    talking clips' caption style: hormozi (default), mrbeast, karaoke, box, minimal, neon
      --bed SONG      music under talking clips, ducked under the voice (link, file or trend:N)
      --speed K       footage speed (talking clips: speech speed), 0.5 slow motion, 2 double
      --velocity      speed ramps on every longer shot: slow, rushing, slow onto the beat
      --fx LIST       hits on the hardest beats: shake, flash, rgb, glitch, invert, blur, freeze, zoom
      --lines N       N sentences from the videos' own words on screen (music and preset edits)
      --line TEXT     a line of your own on screen (repeat for more); replaces the picked ones
      --blur-faces    blur faces   --blur-box x,y,w,h  blur a region (fractions of the frame)
      --stabilize  --denoise  --smooth  --fps N  --codec h264|h265|prores|av1  --quality draft|high|master
      --export LIST   timelines beside each clip for other editors: fcpxml, edl, srt
      --no-jumpcuts   keep the pauses   --no-sfx  no whoosh/boom   --no-hook  no title on top
      --no-captions   no burned-in captions
      --no-llm        pick moments from viewer signals only

ENVIRONMENT
  EDITS_LLM            command that reads a prompt on stdin (default: claude -p)
  EDITS_WHISPER_MODEL  path to a ggml whisper model

Your taste lives in ~/.config/edits/taste.md. Edit it freely.
