Design kit

One mark, one red, one typeface. Anything not listed here is not part of the system.

The mark

A letter E drawn with one stroke width on a 96-unit grid, then cut on a slant. The cut half is red and slips down half a stroke: a splice, a jump cut.

  • Clear space: one stroke width on every side.
  • Never outline it, shadow it, tilt it, recolour the halves or put it in a circle.
  • On photos or mid-tones, use the icon (mark on an ink tile) instead of the bare mark.

Colour

Red is the cut. It appears once per view: the punchline caption, the sliced half of the mark. Never red on gray, never red next to another saturated colour.

Grades

  • bw: no colour, contrast +12%. The default.
  • dark: saturation 75%, slightly crushed.
  • teal: cool shadows, saturation 80%.
  • none: the source as it is.
