# Post to TikTok

Signs in to TikTok once in a window of its own and posts your clips there from your own account: edits connect tiktok, then edits post --to tiktok.

```sh
edits connect tiktok                     # once: sign in, in a window of its own
edits post clip.mp4 --to tiktok --caption "..."
edits follow <channel|feed> --post tiktok   # every new video, made and posted
```

It runs only on tiktok, from the person's own account. Ask before posting anything they did not ask to post.
