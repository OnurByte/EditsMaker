# Post to Instagram

Signs in to Instagram once in a window of its own and posts your clips there from your own account: edits connect instagram, then edits post --to instagram.

```sh
edits connect instagram                     # once: sign in, in a window of its own
edits post clip.mp4 --to instagram --caption "..."
edits follow <channel|feed> --post instagram   # every new video, made and posted
```

It runs only on instagram, from the person's own account. Ask before posting anything they did not ask to post.
