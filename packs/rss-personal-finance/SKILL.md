# Personal finance

Personal finance from 30 feeds, Afford Anything®, Afford Anything®, Budgets Are Sexy: videos from their newest posts.

```sh
edits rss personal-finance --list                    # the newest posts (--json for programs)
edits rss personal-finance -n 3                       # a video for each of the 3 newest
edits rss personal-finance --feed all -n 5 --preset sinema-bant
edits follow personal-finance --post tiktok --every 30 # RSS in, video made, posted out (edits watch runs it)
```
