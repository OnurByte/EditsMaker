# Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrency from 27 feeds, Bitcoin & Crypto Trading: Ledger Cast, Bitcoin - The Currency of the Internet, Bitcoin Audible: videos from their newest posts.

```sh
edits rss cryptocurrency --list                    # the newest posts (--json for programs)
edits rss cryptocurrency -n 3                       # a video for each of the 3 newest
edits rss cryptocurrency --feed all -n 5 --preset sinema-bant
edits follow cryptocurrency --post tiktok --every 30 # RSS in, video made, posted out (edits watch runs it)
```
