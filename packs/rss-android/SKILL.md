# Android

Android from 16 feeds, All About Android (Audio), Android, Android: videos from their newest posts.

```sh
edits rss android --list                    # the newest posts (--json for programs)
edits rss android -n 3                       # a video for each of the 3 newest
edits rss android --feed all -n 5 --preset sinema-bant
edits follow android --post tiktok --every 30 # RSS in, video made, posted out (edits watch runs it)
```
